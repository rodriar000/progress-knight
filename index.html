A chronicle of many lives

The Amulet

Some things only reveal themselves with time.

The relic in your hands The copper amulet Unmarked, yet strangely familiar.

I
Age 25

A curious find

You stumble across a strange looking amulet on your 25th birthday. It does not look like it has any worth on the market given that it's made from cheap-looking copper. However, you feel a weird urge to keep it, so you slip it into your pocket for safekeeping.

II
Age 45

The first marking

On your 45th birthday, the amulet shivers uncontrollably in your pocket. When you take it out, it falls still. A strange etching has appeared at its centre.

III
Age 65

The eye opens

The shivering returns. A living eye emerges from the copper. With the years catching up to you, you consider reaching out and touching it.

A new life

You lose: current levels and coins. You keep: your highest levels, granting job and skill xp multipliers of 1 + max level / 10 in future lives.

The amulet may transform further after two whole centuries...

IV
Age 200

The voice within

An ominous hum rises from the amulet. A mouth emerges and cackles: “So you've made it this far... Are you ready to embrace evil?”

A darker beginning

You lose: levels, coins and highest levels. You gain: a new line of skills and evil for future lives.
